About

Developer, marketer, founder.

I’m Mohd Ahmed Raza — a full-stack web developer, SEO and digital marketing specialist, and AI developer based in Nagpur, India. I’m also a commerce student at Raja Shankar Shah University, which means I think about websites the way a business owner does: they have to bring in results, not just look good.

My work started early. I scored 92/100 in Information Technology in Class X, and in 2022 I co-founded a dermatology skin care clinic. I built its website, set up its Google Business Profile, led its branding and ran its marketing — contributing to a 19× increase in patient flow within a year. That experience taught me what actually moves the needle for a local business.

Today I combine that hands-on marketing experience with development and AI. I built Zain:AI, a chatbot website with voice input, text-to-speech and memory, and I completed IIT Madras’s AI for Management course, earning a Silver Medal. I’ve also written a book, published on Amazon.

I lead as well as build: I’ve been Vice School Captain, Student of the Year, and today I serve as E-Cell President of the Post Graduate College, Seoni.
